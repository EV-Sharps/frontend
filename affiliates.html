Program terms and sharing

New paid customers receive 20% off their first month. You earn 20% of eligible subscription payments after discounts and before tax for 12 months from their first payment. Failed payments, credits, and refunded amounts do not earn commission. A disputed invoice earns no commission while the dispute is open or lost; a won dispute restores eligible earnings. Plan changes do not restart the 12-month period.

Referral links last 30 days. An entered partner code takes priority before the first payment. No self-referrals or discount stacking.

Disclose your relationship when sharing: “I earn a commission when you subscribe using my code.” Promote the research tools without promises of betting profits.
