How profit, middles and capacity work

An arb has positive net profit in every modeled outcome. Cash stakes are returned on winning bets. The ranking uses the lowest outcome profit divided by the total cash used, including applicable fees. Unused budget stays in your balance.

A middle uses different lines on the same stat. Over 40 and under 45 both win at 41–44; a result of 40 or 45 can push one side. All outcomes, including pushes, count toward the worst-case return. The both-win profit is a conditional payout, not an expected return.

Quoted exchange liquidity must cover each order when “Require quoted capacity” is selected. Ordinary sportsbook limits can be unknown and need checking in the betslip. Prices and capacity are not reserved; multiple opportunities may use the same funds or liquidity.

Quotes older than 10 minutes and games that have started are excluded. Feed timestamps do not guarantee every individual selection was just updated. Open-market links may start on a different side or line; check the exact selections and matching settlement rules on both betslips.
