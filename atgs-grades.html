How grades workFixed before reviewing results
A Strong agreement
At least +5% EV against both reference families, with positive EV against every included reference.
B Positive agreement
At least +2% EV against both reference families.
C Small edge
Positive EV against both reference families, below the B threshold.
D No shared edge
At least one reference family has EV of zero or below.
U Insufficient evidence
Required sharp or liquid prediction market evidence is missing. Unknown depth never qualifies a prediction market.

These are grades of the quoted price, not the probability a player scores. High EV does not mean a high win rate. Grade boundaries remain fixed; a profitable historical A record is not guaranteed.

Historical archives may lack quote timestamps or depth. Their results are historical context, with missing required liquidity left unrated. Forward tracking freezes the first fully rateable A–D selection successfully published before the game. Unrated observations do not reserve a record; later results cannot change the original grade.
