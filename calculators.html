How it works

Use two opposing outcomes where exactly one bet wins. Payout includes the winning stake. Stakes are rounded to cents, and Total Payout uses the smaller of the two returns.

Profit is Total Payout minus Total Stake. The percentage is profit divided by payout. This calculator uses cash bets and excludes fees, pushes, and voids.
