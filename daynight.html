Day/Night Split Analysis

Does hitting better in day games actually carry over year to year, or is it just noise?

▶ Do Day Game Hitters Stay Day Game Hitters? (click to expand)
A real day-game trait would mean the same batters consistently outperform at day every year. Instead, most batters flip their preference almost every other season.
▶ Individual Batter Day/Night Splits (click to expand)
The gap shifts around year to year with no real pattern. It's not like guys are consistently better or worse in one context.
▶ Sample Size Reality Check (click to expand)
~100 day ABs per season isn't a lot. The margin of error is around ±75 OPS points - most splits you see are just random variation dressed up as a pattern.
