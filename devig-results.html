How to read these results

ROI is net profit divided by the number of settled offers, including pushes, at a flat 1 unit of risk. Pending and ungraded offers do not enter the denominator. The summary and breakdowns use exactly one reference and method.

Different strategies can qualify overlapping offers. Comparing them does not create independent samples. Odds, book and market rankings are descriptive results from this window; small samples and selecting past winners can overstate a repeatable edge.
