How conversion and liquidity work

Use the bonus on YES (1+ home run, goal or touchdown), then cash on the same player's NO outcome at another book. The bonus stake is not returned. Conversion is the smaller of the cash retained in the two outcomes, divided by your bonus amount.

Each play uses the best hedge that fits your amount, liquidity setting and cash cap. Rankings include the modeled hedge fees and stake rounding. A larger bonus can change the chosen hedge book or remove a play entirely.

Kalshi estimates use whole contracts and fees rounded up to cents: 3.5% × contracts × price × (1 − price) for MLB home runs, and 7% for NFL and NHL. ProphetX includes a 2% fee on net winnings. Novig sizing reserves $0.50 because its quoted depth is rounded to whole dollars. Displayed prices are rounded, so actual order costs can differ. Fee sources: Kalshi and ProphetX.

Verified capacity is the quoted stake available at the displayed price; it is not reserved for you. Missing limits stay unknown. Capacity is checked separately for each play; multiple plays may compete for the same funds or liquidity. No missing NO price is inferred.

Quotes more than 10 minutes old and games that have started are excluded. Timestamps reflect a book's feed update, not a guaranteed update of every selection. Check both betslips, fees, bonus eligibility and settlement or void rules before placing anything. Open-market links may start on YES; select NO for the hedge.
