How conversion and liquidity work

Use the bonus on the displayed bonus side, then cash on the exact opposing side at another book. NBA props can use either Over or Under; game and period lines must match the team, line and period. Scorer markets must match the player and scoring outcome. First-basket matches currently require FanDuel or Bet365 first-score rules, including free throws, and a quoted opposite outcome. Markets with incompatible or unknown settlement rules are excluded.

The bonus stake is not returned on a win. Conversion is the lowest cash retained across the applicable outcomes, divided by your bonus amount. When a whole-number line can push or a market can tie, the displayed Push/tie outcome is included in that minimum. Cash stakes are returned on a push; bonus treatment depends on the book. Check the play's settlement note.

Each play uses the best hedge that fits your amount, liquidity setting and cash cap. Rankings include the modeled hedge fees and stake rounding. A larger bonus can change the chosen hedge book or remove a play entirely.

Kalshi estimates use whole contracts and fees rounded up to cents: 3.5% × contracts × price × (1 − price) for MLB home runs, and 7% for NFL, NHL and NBA. ProphetX includes a 2% fee on net winnings. Novig sizing reserves $0.50 because its quoted depth is rounded to whole dollars. Displayed prices are rounded, so actual order costs can differ. Fee sources: Kalshi and ProphetX.

Verified capacity is the quoted stake available at the displayed price; it is not reserved for you. Missing limits stay unknown. Capacity is checked separately for each play; multiple plays may compete for the same funds or liquidity. Missing opposite-side prices are never inferred.

Quotes more than 10 minutes old and games that have started are excluded. Timestamps reflect a book's feed update, not a guaranteed update of every selection. Check both betslips, fees, bonus eligibility and settlement or void rules before placing anything. Open-market links may select the other side, including team and period markets; match the exact side and line shown for each bet.
