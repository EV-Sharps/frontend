A few good questions.

Visit the FAQ ↗
What does positive expected value mean?

A price has positive expected value when its potential return exceeds what your estimated probability implies. The estimate depends on your references and devig method. A positive EV number is a model output, not a promise that a bet will win.

Can I choose which books I compare?

Yes. Supported prop pages let you customize devig books and weights, and filter the offers you research. Available tools and sportsbook coverage vary by page and membership.

Are the example prices on this page live?

No. These examples show how price comparison and order book depth work. Open a prop page for its latest published prices, check the update time, and verify an offer at the sportsbook before betting.
