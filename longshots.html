How to read this watchlistModel and research context

Research matches use last-season results for the same betting book, reference and method. Opening and closing samples are evaluated separately, not pooled. This is a historical screen, not a profit forecast.

Stored history covers the original +1000 to +3000, EV ≥ 0 opening and closing cohorts. Many strategies were inspected; neither season is an untouched holdout. The live screen excludes a problematic region of one-sided reference prices, so the stored ROI does not precisely backtest today's guarded screen.

Position, recent shots and team goals are context. Custom filters do not recalculate the historical cohorts. Details shows the player's alternative book offers; research evidence belongs to the specific book and reference.

Opening means the saved approximately 10 a.m. Eastern baseline; closing means 30 minutes before scheduled start. These cohorts can overlap. Historical stakes were one unit per selection.
