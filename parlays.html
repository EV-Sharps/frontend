How the fair price is calculated

We remove the margin from each player's over/under 0.5 home run market, multiply the three fair probabilities, then convert the result to American odds. The calculations use full precision before rounding the display.

Power, multiplicative and probit remove the margin differently. The lowest-probability option takes the smallest of those estimates for each leg; it is a sensitivity check, not a confidence bound.

All three legs must have both prices at the same reference book. Missing unders are never inferred. Quotes older than 10 minutes are excluded; timestamps describe the book's feed update, not a guaranteed update of every selection.

These estimates assume independent outcomes across different matchups. Same-game and doubleheader combinations are excluded. Fair odds are not offered parlay prices. Actual eligibility, starting-player requirements and void rules depend on the sportsbook.
