    How the fair price is calculated

    Estimated book prices multiply the decimal odds for all two to four legs at that book, then convert the total to American odds. Books are ranked by EV using your selected devig method and the lowest complete parlay probability from your chosen fair value books. A reference must price every leg to be included. Without a complete selected reference, EV is unavailable and books are ordered by odds. A betting book needs a current over price for every leg; prices from different books are never mixed into one parlay.

    For fair odds, we remove the margin from each player's over/under 0.5 home run market, multiply the fair probabilities, then convert the result to American odds. The calculations use full precision before rounding the display.

    Power, multiplicative and probit remove the margin differently. The lowest-probability option takes the smallest of those estimates for each leg; it is a sensitivity check, not a confidence bound.

    Every leg must have both prices at the same reference book to calculate fair odds. Missing unders are never inferred. Quotes older than 10 minutes are excluded; timestamps describe the book's feed update, not a guaranteed update of every selection.

    These estimates assume independent outcomes across different matchups. Same-game and doubleheader combinations are excluded. Fair odds are not offered parlay prices. Actual eligibility, starting-player requirements and void rules depend on the sportsbook.
