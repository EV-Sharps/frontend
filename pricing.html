A few helpful answers.

Want to learn more about the tools?
Visit the full FAQ ↗

What does Sharp add over Analyst?

Sharp includes everything in Analyst, plus live pages, expanded player props and alternate lines, and main markets such as moneylines, spreads, and totals.

Which live pages are included?

Sharp includes live pages for MLB, NFL, NBA, and NHL. Available games, books, and markets depend on the sport and current coverage; a page may be empty when no live markets are available.

Can I try the site before subscribing?

Yes. Free access includes selected pages and research previews. Choose Explore free to start with the strikeouts page, or use the Pages menu to look around.

How do I change or cancel my plan?

Sign in and open your profile to access the billing portal. You can manage, change, or cancel your subscription there.
