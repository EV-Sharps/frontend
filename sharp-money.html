What the numbers mean

Supporting stake is the money risked by resting orders that accept a shorter price on the same outcome than your sportsbook offers. It is distinct from the larger opposite-side stake someone could take.

Price advantage compares the sportsbook payout with the payout implied by those qualifying orders, weighted by contract payout. It is a price comparison, not an estimated probability or EV. Fees and settlement rules can change the comparison.

The exchange filter recalculates support and price advantage from that exchange. Saved history shows all-exchange captures for the specific betting book; it is not continuous monitoring and does not prove orders stayed available between captures.

Circa and Pinnacle are reference books, never betting offers here. The best qualifying offer is selected after applying your filters.
