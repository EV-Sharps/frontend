Each slot has ~50–80 ABs per year. A ±0.100 OPS swing is meaningless noise at that sample size - you can't reliably distinguish a .650 OPS slot from a .750 OPS slot.
▶ 'Pitchers Fixed Their Weakspot' - Or Regression to the mean? (click to expand)
Pitchers don't consciously fix slot #2.
If 2nd-worst inverts at the same rate as worst, the cause is math (regression to mean), not coaching.
▶ Did Pitchers Improve Their Weakspot More Than Other Slots? (Control: 2nd-worst) (click to expand)
Regression to mean alone predicts the worst slot improves the most. The 2nd-worst slot is the control group - if it also improves substantially, you're seeing math, not coaching.
